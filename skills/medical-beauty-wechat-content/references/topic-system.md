## Demand Signals To Check

Use publicly available information when possible:

- Xiaohongshu/search snippets and comment sections: prioritize repeated questions in comments over the author's main post. Look for users asking price, where to buy, whether it is genuine, how to use, whether it suits their skin/project, side effects, recovery, and alternative products.
- Weibo: more colloquial pain points and user phrasing.
- WeChat competitor posts: titles, collection structures, comparison topics.
- User's own account data: reads, scans, private messages, add-friend count, orders.
- Seasonality: summer oil/acne/acid/brightening; winter hydration/repair/anti-aging.

If real-time platform access is blocked, state the limitation and use public search results plus the user's screenshots and account data.

## Product-Demand Chain Model

When judging whether a medical-beauty product is worth writing, use this full chain instead of only asking whether the product is "hot":

`doctor/institution push -> C-side hears product name -> Xiaohongshu/Video Channel/WeChat content grows -> users search for authenticity, price, suitability, risks, alternatives, and channels -> supply is opaque, scarce, expensive, or hard to buy -> private-domain consultation and orders rise`

Interpret the chain:

- Doctor/institution push: new product launch, official training, academic conference, white paper, doctor case posts, institution posters, consultant scripts.
- C-side hears product name: users ask "what is this", "is it the same as another name", "why is my doctor recommending it".
- Content grows: more Xiaohongshu notes, Video Channel clips, WeChat articles, institution activity pages, doctor explainers, comparison posts.
- Search questions appear: price, where to buy, whether genuine, specification, anti-counterfeit, suited areas, recovery, side effects, alternatives, "A vs B".
- Supply/channel friction: hard to find, out of stock, channel price opaque, high institution price, no obvious e-commerce path, mixed names, hard-to-verify authenticity.
- Private-domain conversion: users add WeChat to ask price, stock, channel, suitability, one-piece dropshipping, small wholesale, or sendable customer education material.

High-value hot topic signs:

- Product has 2+ names or Chinese/English names that users confuse.
- Product is newly approved, newly launched, or newly pushed by institutions.
- Product has high institution price and opaque channel price.
- Product has clear comparison targets, such as CaHA vs CaHA, filler vs filler, acid A vs acid B.
- Users in comments ask purchase/channel/price/authenticity/suitability questions.
- The user's own article gets high "搜一搜" share, repeated long-tail reads, or add-friend inquiries.
- Product is scarce, sold out, hard to find, or not easy to buy through normal e-commerce.

Low-value hot topic signs:

- Only broad views but no price/channel/suitability questions.
- The product is easy to buy on e-commerce with transparent price.
- The topic is broad education with no specific product search intent.
- Low margin plus high consultation cost and weak repeat/add-on potential.

## Search Checklist For Hot Topics

When the user asks to "抓取", "查热度", "为什么突然火", or "做热门选题", check:

- Official/regulatory: NMPA approval, brand launch, distributor announcement, product registration, official training.
- Institution side: clinic activity pages, doctor posts, conference reports, white papers, institution package names, project posters.
- Xiaohongshu: note titles and especially comments. Prioritize repeated comment questions over blogger wording.
- WeChat search: competitor article titles, whether many posts use "产品名 + 区别/禁忌/使用方法/真假/拿货".
- Video Channel/Douyin/Bilibili if available: doctor explanation, institution cases, consultant education clips.
- Supply signal: out-of-stock, price fluctuation, "哪里买", "求渠道", "正品吗", "能不能拿货".
- User-side data: the user's reads, 搜一搜 share, shares, follows, add-friend count, active inquiries, orders.

If Xiaohongshu or Video Channel comments cannot be directly accessed, clearly say so and ask the user to provide screenshots. Do not pretend to have seen complete comment sections.

## Post-Performance Review Method

For each published WeChat article, review after 1 day, 7 days, and 30 days.

Record:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Article title | Exact title used |
| Product/project | Specific product, not broad category |
| Publish date | Date |
| Article type | Single product / comparison / collection / aftercare / B-side |
| Search keyword | Keyword intended for source tracking |
| 1-day reads | Early signal |
| 7-day reads | Main performance window |
| 30-day reads | Long-tail search value |
| 搜一搜 reads and ratio | Search intent strength |
| Shares | Whether users forward it as decision material |
| New followers/add-friends | Lead volume |
| Active speakers | Users who ask after adding |
| Price askers | Conversion intent |
| Orders | Real conversion |
| Customer type | Personal use / small agent / salon / institution staff |
| Profit | Estimated actual profit, not just revenue |
| Consultation cost | Whether many users ask but do not convert |
| Stock/channel status | In stock, scarce, out of stock, hard to source |
| External demand reason | New launch, institution push, shortage, season, controversy, comparison trend |
| Next action | Rewrite, comparison follow-up, B-side post, abandon, or keep long-tail |

Review logic:

- High reads + high 搜一搜 + high shares: strong search decision article; create follow-up comparison and channel/anti-counterfeit posts.
- High reads + low add-friend: content answers too much or product lacks purchase friction; add a clearer lead window or switch to comparison.
- High add-friend + low active speaking: lead window may be too passive; use a softer first-message script and source keyword.
- High ask-price + low conversion: likely price-sensitive or being used to check market price; avoid public price, ask use case first, and offer "个人自用/客户搭配/机构拿货" paths.
- Low reads + high conversion: niche but valuable; keep writing long-tail and B-side versions.
- Low reads + low conversion: deprioritize unless margin is high or stock is strategic.

Recommended review output:

1. What happened in the data.
2. Whether traffic was platform search, product trend, season, shortage/channel, or title structure.
3. Whether traffic had business value.
4. What exact follow-up topics to write.
5. What to change in lead window and private-domain follow-up.

## WeChat Search Intent Bias

Assume most WeChat Official Account search users are not broad-interest readers. They usually already have a product, project, or channel intention in mind.

Prioritize topics with explicit product/channel intent:

- Product name + 使用方法
- Product name + 适合人群
- Product name + 拿货/渠道
- Product name + 真假/防伪/规格
- Product A vs Product B
- Product name + 搭配
- Product name + 注意事项

Deprioritize broad educational topics unless they clearly bridge into a product the user can sell:

- 泛泛的“水光和光子怎么选”
- 泛泛的“术后修复合集”
- 泛泛的“美容院怎么经营”

These can be used only when reframed around a specific product or channel question.

- 夏天油皮控油祛痘，刷酸和水光怎么搭

- 富勒烯水光 6 款怎么选
- 夏季油皮水光合集
- 术后修复产品合集

- 美容院刷酸项目怎么搭配产品
- 小工作室不想压货，某个具体水光/刷酸产品怎么少量拿货
- 一件代发适合哪些产品

## Product-Specific Notes

- Tirzepatide/weight-loss drugs: high demand and conversion, but high compliance risk. Avoid "zero side effects", guaranteed weight loss, and self-medication guidance. Frame as information整理 and consult professionals.
- 博乐达/科瑞达: summer acid-peeling, oil/acne, salon B-side potential. Good for project matching and aftercare.
- 润致娃娃/械三: high traffic, lower margin. Use as entry product and add repair/combo recommendations.
- 优法兰/CaHA: professional trust and institution/doctor lead potential. Write as decision/comparison, not only product manual.
- 普丽颜/普丽妍: high inquiry but weak conversion. Use to filter budget and redirect to suitable alternatives.
